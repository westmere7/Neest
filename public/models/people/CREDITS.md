# People models

Baked by `npm run people` (tools/bake-people.mjs) from:

- **Ultimate Modular Men Pack** and **Ultimate Modular Women Pack** by Quaternius
  (https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularmen.html, https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularwomen.html),
  CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain). Fetched as GLB copies from https://github.com/nikitapage10/models (CC0).

Each figure is re-posed, re-scaled (Asian sizing first) and split into material slots; original colours are not used.
